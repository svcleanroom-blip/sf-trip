Packing list

Mid-October SF: highs around 70°F, evenings in the 50s, and windy by the water. Pack in layers.

  • 3 show outfits: polos or button-downs + 2 pairs of chinos
  • Your most comfortable walking sneakers (worn in, not new)
  • Turf shoes or flat sneakers for soccer
  • Athletic shorts or joggers + a dri-fit tee
  • Small towel + a plastic bag for sweaty clothes
  • Dark jeans + 2 casual tees or a henley
  • Hoodie or fleece
  • Windbreaker or light rain shell
  • Beanie (for Lands End)
  • Small backpack or sling bag
  • Phone charger + portable battery
  • ID, show badge, Clipper card in Apple Wallet
  • A few dollars in quarters (pool table + arcade)
  • Refillable water bottle
Expo hours: Tue & Wed 10am–5pm, Thu 10am–4pm (you arrive 9am). At night, rideshare or Muni back to the hotel instead of walking through the Tenderloin.
